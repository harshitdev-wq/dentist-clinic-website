About the clinic

A dental studio designed around how you feel

Founded in 2008, Lumière Dental was built on a simple belief: exceptional dentistry should feel less like a procedure and more like a retreat. Our Madison Avenue studio pairs museum-calm interiors with the most advanced clinical technology in the city.

From digital 3D scanning that replaces uncomfortable impressions, to sedation options for anxious patients, every detail is engineered for comfort — without ever compromising on precision.

  • Digital 3D smile design & impression-free scanning
  • Sedation & anxiety-free dentistry programs
  • Biocompatible, metal-free materials
  • Board-certified specialists in every discipline
